About

Christian Van Meter is a Christian speaker and content creator who helps women grow in their relationship with Jesus through Scripture, testimony, and practical encouragement. Her desire is to inspire others to know God deeply, trust Him fully, and boldly follow His leading in every season of life.

After serving as a registered nurse for a decade and working in healthcare for fifteen years. God asked her to step away from her career in early 2020. As the COVID-19 pandemic unfolded, marking a season that would profoundly shape her faith. In that transition, she realized how much of her identity had been tied to her career and began seeking the Lord more deeply than ever before. In December 2021, God called her to start a YouTube channel. She stepped out in obedience to share biblical encouragement online, and what began as an act of faith has grown into a ministry focused on pointing people to Jesus and His Word.

Inspired by Mark 16, her mission is to share the gospel of Jesus Christ, see lives transformed by the Holy Spirit, and walk in obedient faith as God moves through His people. Christian carries a deep hunger for God and a sincere heart to see revival. She longs for lives transformed by the power of the Holy Spirit and homes restored by the truth of God’s Word.

Christian has been married for fourteen years, is a mother of two daughters, and serves in her local church. Her prayer is that every person would encounter Jesus in a life-changing way and step into the revival God desires to bring to our families, churches, and communities.

Family is one of God's greatest blessings in Christian's life. She has been married for fourteen years, is the mother of two daughters, and three dogs. She treasures spending time with her family while serving Christ together.
